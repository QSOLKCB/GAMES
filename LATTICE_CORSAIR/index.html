      How to fly

      The galaxy

      The five empires of the Sovereign Lattice are at war, and the war keeps moving while you fly: every two days the empires take a turn. Their colonies are the stations you dock at.

      Trade

      Stations sell what their world produces cheaply and pay well for what it consumes. War fronts pay extra for alloys, fuel, medicine and munitions. Selling a lot at once lowers the price.

      Contracts

      Bounties, couriers, patrols and war contracts pay credits and standing. Missed deadlines cost standing.

      Standing

      Killing outlaws earns favour; attacking an empire's ships angers that empire and pleases its enemies. Fall too low and its patrols attack and its stations refuse you.

      Outfit

      Shipyards sell what their empire has researched. Better hulls, weapons, deflectors, boosters, pods and drones change how you fly.

      Insurance

      If you are destroyed, the insurer returns you to your last port with a loaner, keeps a share of your credits and your cargo is gone.
