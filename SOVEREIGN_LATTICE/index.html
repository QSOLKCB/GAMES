How to play

Explore

Stars start unexplored. Scouts and colonies reveal systems. Ancient beacons grant research to the first empire that finds them.

Expand

Send a Settler Ark to a world your people can live on, then press Colonize. Colonization research opens new climates, outposts on gas giants and asteroids, and arcologies.

Exploit

Queue facilities and ships at your colonies. Minerals build, energy pays upkeep (a shortfall halves research and construction), labs make research.

Exterminate

Fleets fight automatically when hostiles share a system. Knock a colony's defences to zero, then land troops (Boarding Barges) to capture it. Capitals are fortified.

Design

Every ship is a hull plus components. Components scale with research, so existing ships upgrade as you learn. Bigger hulls need Armor research.

Win

Eliminate every rival, or lead the score when the turn limit arrives.
